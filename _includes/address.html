{%- comment -%} Postal address, lightly hardened against scraping — same bar and same reasoning as email.html. Hidden separators are inserted between the words of the street and into the middle of the postal code, so the raw source contains no contiguous "Street 1, 12345 City" for a pattern matcher and no bare \d{5} postal code, while the rendered text, screen-reader output and copy-paste are all unchanged (display:none content is excluded from selections). Note for future edits: do NOT entity-encode the digits. Chained Liquid `replace` filters re-enter their own output — encoding "1" as 1 leaves a 4 and a 9 for the next filter to mangle. This approach has no such interaction. § 5 DDG requires the address be permanently available and easily recognisable. Nothing here depends on JavaScript. The separators are hidden with an inline `display:none`, so in the pathological case of a client that ignores style attributes entirely the address degrades to "c/o· COCENTER / Koppoldstr.· 1 / 86·551 Aichach" — still unambiguous to a human reader, which is the standard the provision sets. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign L = site.legal -%} {%- assign street_words = L.street | split: ' ' -%} {%- assign care_words = L.care_of | split: ' ' -%} {%- assign zip_head = L.postal_code | slice: 0, 2 -%} {%- assign zip_rest = L.postal_code | slice: 2, 10 -%} {%- capture sep -%}·{%- endcapture -%} {%- if L.care_of and L.care_of != '' -%} {% for w in care_words %}{{ w }}{% unless forloop.last %}{{ sep }} {% endunless %}{% endfor %}
