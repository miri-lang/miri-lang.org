The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no tracking scripts, and makes no requests to third-party servers. Fonts, styles, scripts, images and the GPU demos are all served from this domain. Nothing is stored on your device and nothing is read from it, so there is no consent banner to click away — there is nothing to consent to.

What remains is unavoidable: to send you this page, the server has to receive a request from your device, and that request is logged. The rest of this document explains that in the detail the GDPR requires.

No data protection officer has been appointed. This is a private, non-commercial website processing personal data on a very small scale, so the thresholds in Art. 37 GDPR and § 38 BDSG are not met.

Server log data

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. When you open a page, your browser necessarily transmits technical information which those providers process to deliver the page and keep the service secure:

  • IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • referring URL, if your browser sends one
  • browser type and version, and operating system

Purpose: delivering the website, ensuring stability and security, and defending against attacks and abuse.
Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is operating a functioning, secure website.
Retention: this data is held by the hosting and CDN providers under their own retention policies and is not merged by me with any other data, nor used to identify individual visitors.

Website analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It is cookieless: it sets no cookies, writes nothing to local storage, and does not create a cross-site identifier or a device fingerprint. It records the page requested, the referring page, and coarse technical characteristics such as screen size, and aggregates them into visit statistics.

Purpose: understanding which pages are read, so I can write more useful documentation.
Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR — a legitimate interest in understanding the reach of my own writing, weighed against the very limited nature of the data, which does not identify you and is not used to build a profile.

Fonts

All typefaces are served from this domain. They are not loaded from Google Fonts or any other external service, so opening a page transmits nothing to Google and creates no connection to a third-party font provider.

GPU demos and code examples

The demos on the home page and under /gpu-demos/ are Miri programs compiled ahead of time and shipped as static files from this domain. They render locally in your browser through WebGL/WebGPU, on your own graphics hardware. They send nothing anywhere: no result, no telemetry, no timing data and no information about your device leaves your browser, and nothing you type or click in a demo is transmitted or stored.

Cookies and local storage

None. This site sets no cookies of any kind — not for analytics, not for preferences, not for sessions — and stores nothing in local storage or session storage. Accordingly, no consent under § 25 TDDDG is required, and there is no consent banner.

Links to GitHub

Pages here link to the Miri repository on GitHub. Those are ordinary links: nothing is requested from GitHub until you click one, and no GitHub script, button or widget is embedded on this site. Once you follow such a link, GitHub's own privacy policy applies to what happens there.

Contacting me by email

If you email me, your address and the content of your message are processed solely to handle your enquiry.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, and Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your message concerns a prospective or existing agreement.
Retention: until your enquiry is settled, unless statutory retention periods apply. You can ask me to delete the correspondence at any time.

Comments

This site has no comment function and no comment service is loaded. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the feature goes live.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — confirmation of whether I process data about you, and a copy of it (Art. 15)
  • Rectification — correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure — deletion of your data (Art. 17)
  • Restriction — limitation of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability — your data in a structured, machine-readable format (Art. 20)
  • Objection — to processing based on legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21)

To exercise any of these, email {% include email.html address=contact_email %}. Where processing is ever based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority under Art. 77 GDPR — in the EU member state of your residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.
